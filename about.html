ABOUT LIFELINK

Connecting Donors, Saving Lives in Real Time.

Our Mission

LifeLink is a community-driven emergency blood donation platform dedicated to bridging the critical gap between voluntary blood donors, blood banks, and patients facing urgent medical emergencies.

What We Do

  • Instant Emergency Requests: Broadcast urgent blood requests to active local donors without delay.
  • Verified Donor Directory: Maintain an organized, real-time database of ready and accessible donors.
  • Blood Bank Search: Enable quick lookup of nearby authorized blood storage centers.
  • 24/7 AI Assistance: Provide round-the-clock guidance to assist users during high-stress critical situations.

Our Vision

We envision a seamless, tech-enabled healthcare community where no life is compromised due to a delay in discovering compatible, life-saving blood supplies.
